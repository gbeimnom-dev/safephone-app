Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

SafePhone Backup is a free web app that saves your contacts and call history to your own account, so you can open them from any phone or computer. This page explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

InformationWhy we keep it
Your email address and passwordTo create your account and let you sign in. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and we never see it in plain text.
Contacts you add or import (name, phone number, email)To show them to you when you sign in.
Call history you import (name, number, type, date, duration)To show it to you when you sign in.
The time of your last backupTo show your backup status.

We do not read your phone. The app only keeps what you type in or import yourself. We do not use advertising or analytics tools on this site.

Other people’s information

Contacts and calls include details about other people. Please only upload information you are entitled to keep, and keep your account password private.

Where your data is kept

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). The website is hosted by Netlify, and the code is kept on GitHub. These providers may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run their services. Each has its own privacy policy.

Who can see your data

  • Database rules allow each signed-in person to read and write only their own records. Other users cannot open your contacts or calls.
  • Your data is sent over a secure (https) connection.
  • Your data is not end-to-end encrypted. The person who runs this project manages the database and can technically open stored data in the Firebase console. It is only opened to fix problems, and it is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or ask us to delete your account.

Your choices

  • Delete your saved contacts and calls: sign in, open Settings, and tap Delete all saved data.
  • Delete your account, or ask what we hold about you: email us at the address below and we will respond. Please write from the email address you signed up with.
  • Correct information: edit or delete any contact or call inside the app.
  • Reset your password: use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.

Cookies and browser storage

To keep you signed in, Firebase stores a sign-in token in your browser. We do not use tracking cookies.

Children

SafePhone Backup is not meant for children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you use it.

Your rights

We aim to respect your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws, including the right to access, correct and delete your information.

Changes to this page

If we change how the app works, we will update this page and its date.
